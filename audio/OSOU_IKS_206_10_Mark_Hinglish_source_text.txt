Q1 (Unit 1): Indian Knowledge System (IKS) ka concept, main sources, Pramanas
(Epistemology), aur philosophical foundations explain karo.
Introduction
Indian Knowledge System (IKS) India ki hazaaron saal purani gyan parampara hai jo material
growth (Abhyudaya) aur spiritual liberation (Moksha) dono ko saath lekar chalti hai. Yeh sirf
kitabigyan nahi, balki philosophy, science, health aur lifestyle ka ek complete package hai.
Main Sources of IKS
Shruti Literature: Char Ved (Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva), Brahmanas, Aranyakas aur
Upanishads.
Smriti & Shastras: Vedangas, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Puranas, aur Arthashastra ya
Charaka Samhita jaise special granth.
Oral & Heterodox Traditions: Buddhist Tripitaka, Jain Agamas aur Guru-Shishya parampara
se aaya folk/tribal gyan.
Epistemology (Pramana Shastra) & Logic
Sahi gyan (Prama) paane ke liye 6 Pramanas maane jaate hain: Pratyaksha (ankhon se
dekhna), Anumana (logic se guess karna), Shabda (bharosemand vyakti/granth ki baat),
Upamana (tulna karna), Arthapatti (postulation) aur Anupalabdhi (kisi cheez ka na hona).
Tarka (logic) galat soch ko rokta hai.
Philosophical Foundations
Samkhya kehta hai ki duniya Purusha (consciousness) aur Prakriti (matter + 3 Gunas) se bani
hai. Yoga mann ko shaant karne ki technique hai jisse insaan Moksha (dukh aur janam-maran
se azaadi) paa sakta hai.
Conclusion
Aaj ke time mein bhi IKS holistic health, sustainable living aur modern NEP 2020 education ke
liye bahut useful hai.

Q2 (Unit 2): Ancient India ke Mathematics, Astronomy, Metallurgy, Ayurveda,
aur Maritime Trade mein contributions discuss karo.
Introduction
Ancient India ne sirf spirituality nahi, balki exact science, medical surgery aur samudri vyapar
(maritime trade) mein bhi duniya ko lead kiya.
Mathematics & Astronomy
Maths: India ne duniya ko Zero (Shunya) aur decimal system diya. Sulba Sutras (geometry),
Aryabhata (trigonometry, pi), Brahmagupta (negative numbers) aur Bhaskaracharya
(Lilavati) iske main pillars hain.
Astronomy: Surya Siddhanta aur Aryabhatiya mein grahon ki chaal, grahan (eclipses) aur
Panchanga calendar ka bilkul sahi hisab milta hai.
Metallurgy & Ayurveda
Delhi ka bina jang laga Iron Pillar, Wootz steel aur Zawar (Rajasthan) mein zinc distillation India
ki metallurgy ka saboot hai. Health ke liye Ayurveda (Charaka aur Sushruta Samhita) ne
Tridosha (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) balance aur surgery techniques di.
Maritime Trade & Architecture
Lothal dockyard se lekar Tamralipti aur Palur ports tak, Indian merchant guilds (Shreni) Silk aur
Spice Route par vyapar karte the. Yukti Kalpataru mein ship banane ke rules the. Isi samriddhi
se Nagara, Dravida aur Vesara temple styles bane.
Conclusion
India ka scientific aur maritime history practical skill aur global connection ka ek shandaar
example hai.
Q3 (Unit 3): Ancient Indian socio-political thought (Dharma, Rajadharma),
indigenous agriculture, aur environment sustainability ko analyze karo.
Introduction
Ancient India mein politics, kheti (agriculture) aur nature sabhi Dharma—yani sahi niyam aur
kartavya—se jude hue the.
Rajadharma & Statecraft
Rajadharma: Raja ka sabse bada dharm tha Praja-hita (janta ki bhalai), apni khushi nahi.
Saptanga Theory: Kautilya ne Arthashastra mein state ke 7 pillars bataye (Swamin, Amatya,
Janapada, Durga, Kosha, Danda, Mitra) aur Dharmashastra ne nyay (justice) ke rules diye.

Indigenous Agriculture
Krishi-Parashara aur Vrikshayurveda jaise granthon mein baarish ka andaza lagana, beej
(seeds) bachana, crop rotation aur organic khaad (Kunapajala) banane ke tareeke likhe hain.
Environmental Ethics & Water Management
5 elements (Panchamahabhuta) ki pooja aur Atharvaveda ka Prithvi Sukta dharti ko maa
maanta hai. Stepwells (Baolis), talab, Ahar-Pyne aur sacred groves ne paani aur jungle ko
bachaye rakha.
Conclusion
Imandaar shasan, organic kheti aur paani bachane ka yeh purana model aaj ki climate crisis ka
sabse accha solution hai.
Q4 (Unit 4): Panini ki Grammar, Nyaya Logic, Classical Music, aur Yoga ka India
ke intellectual aur cultural growth mein kya role hai?
Introduction
India ki bhasha, logic aur art parampara discipline, deep thinking aur mind-body balance par
tiki hui hai.
Language & Nyaya Logic
Panini ki Ashtadhyayi: Panini ne ~4,000 sutras mein Sanskrit grammar ka ek computer-
code jaisa rule-based system banaya. Patanjali (Mahabhashya) aur Bhartrihari (Sphota
theory) ne isse aage badhaya.
Nyaya Logic: Gautama ke Nyaya Sutras aur baad mein Navya-Nyaya ne debate aur sach ko
prove karne ke 5-step logic rules diye.
Music, Dance & Aesthetics
Samaveda aur Bharata ke Natyashastra se nikla Indian classical music Swara, Raga aur Tala
par chalta hai. Classical dance Abhinaya aur mudras se 9 emotions (Navarasa) jagata hai.
Yoga & Indigenous Games
Patanjali ka Ashtanga Yoga sharir, saans (Pranayama) aur mann ko jodta hai. Chaturanga
(chess), Mallakhamb aur Kabaddi jaise khel dimaag aur body dono ko fit rakhte hain.
Conclusion
Aaj Panini ke rules computer NLP mein use hote hain, aur Yoga poori duniya mein fitness ka
symbol ban chuka hai.

Q5 (Unit 5): Odisha Knowledge System ko explain karo—Odisha ke scholars,
Sadhabas ka maritime trade, aur rural governance highlight karo.
Introduction
Odisha Knowledge System prachin Kalinga, Utkala aur Kosala ki us local wisdom ka naam hai
jisme science, literature aur samudri vyapar sab shamil hai.
Scholars & Learning Centers
Astronomy & Literature: Pathani Samanta (Samanta Chandrasekhar) ne sirf baans
(bamboo) ke tools se Siddhanta Darpana likha. Jayadeva (Gita Govinda), Sarala Das aur
Panchasakha ne Odia mein gyan ko aam logon tak pahunchaya.
Historical Roots: Ratnagiri, Lalitgiri, Udayagiri ki Buddhist universities aur Raja Kharavela
ka Hathigumpha shilalekh Odisha ki shaan hain.
Maritime Trade (Sadhabas) & Rural Governance
Odisha ke Sadhabas (merchants) Tamralipti, Palur aur Manikapatna ports se Boitas (ships)
mein Bali, Java aur Sumatra vyapar karne jaate the—jiski yaad mein aaj bhi Boita Bandana aur
Baliyatra manaya jata hai. Gaon mein Bhagabata Tungi aur gram sabha shiksha aur jhagde
suljhane ka kaam karti thi.
Conclusion
Odisha ka knowledge system scientific talent, samudri himmat aur village-level unity ka
behtareen example hai.
Q6 (Unit 6): Kalinga Temple Architecture, Odissi dance-music, folk traditions,
aur Jagannath Culture ko ek integrated system ke roop mein discuss karo.
Introduction
Odisha ki art aur culture sirf dekhne ki cheez nahi, balki engineering, bhakti aur social equality
ka ek jeeta-jagta system hai.
Kalinga Architecture & Odissi Arts
Temple Styles: Kalinga style ke 3 main types hain—Rekha Deula (Lingaraj, Puri), Pidha
Deula (Konark Jagamohana) aur Khakhara Deula (Vaital Deula), saath hi Udayagiri-
Khandagiri ki rock-cut caves.
Odissi Dance & Music: Mahari aur Gotipua se nikla Odissi apni Tribhanga aur Chowka
postures, aur Mardala ke saath classical ragas ke liye mashhoor hai.
Folk Traditions & Crafts
Pattachitra, Pipili ka Applique, Cuttack ki Tarakasi (silver work), Dhokra aur Mayurbhanj Chhau,
Pala, Daskathia, Dalkhai jaise folk arts Odisha ki pehchan hain.

Jagannath Culture
Jagannath Culture mein tribal, Vedic aur Vaishnav parampara ek saath milti hai. Mahaprasad
(56 Bhog) Ananda Bazar mein bina kisi jaat-paat ke banta jata hai, aur Dwadasa Yatra
(khaaskar Ratha Yatra) sabko ek sutr mein bandhti hai.
Conclusion
Yeh sabhi traditions milkar Odisha ki kala, aastha aur bhaichare ko zinda rakhti hain.
Q7 (Unit 7): Odisha ki tribal wisdom, agricultural cycle, water systems, festivals
(Nuakhai, Ratha Yatra), aur textiles kaise ecological knowledge dikhate hain?
Introduction
Odisha mein nature, kheti aur tyohar (festivals) ek doosre se jude hue hain, jo sustainable living
sikhate hain.
Tribal Ethics & Water Harvesting
Tribal Knowledge: Odisha ki 62 tribes sacred groves (Jahera) aur jadi-booti
(ethnomedicine) ke zariye jungle aur biodiversity bachati hain.
Water Systems: Kata, Munda, Bandha aur Chahala jaise local water tanks baarish ka paani
jama karke kheti ke kaam aate hain.
Agricultural Cycle, Festivals & Food
Kheti Akshaya Tritiya (Akhimuthi Anukula) se shuru hokar nayi fasal ke tyohar Nuakhai par
celebrate hoti hai. Ratha Yatra social unity badhata hai, aur mausam ke hisab se Pakhala,
Dalma, Pitha health aur nutrition dete hain.
Handicrafts & Textile Heritage
Sambalpuri Ikat (Bandha), Bomkai, Kotpad (natural dye) aur Maniabandha sarees eco-
friendly karigari aur mathematical design ka namoona hain.
Conclusion
Odisha ki yeh cultural traditions aaj ke daur mein climate resilience aur green economy ke liye
ek strong model hain.

Introduction
IKS mein Artificial Intelligence (AI) ko jodna purane gyan ko bachane, samajhne aur modern

Key Domain Applications
Health, Science & Farming: AI-based Ayurgenomics (Prakriti aur genetics ko jodna), herbal
drug research, aur purane crop calendars + satellite data se smart farming.
Architecture, Language & Arts: Toote hue mandiron ka 3D AI reconstruction, Panini
grammar se NLP models, Raga/Yoga posture AI, aur Odissi mudras ka digital archive.
Odisha IKS: Odia palm-leaf manuscripts (Pothi) ko OCR se digitize karna aur tribal
languages ko AI se preserve karna.
Ethical & Cultural Guardrails
AI use karte waqt 3 baaton ka dhyan rakhna zaroori hai: cultural authenticity (sacred texts ka
galat matlab na nikle), data sovereignty & IP rights (tribal/local gyan ki chori ya biopiracy na
ho), aur regional languages mein bias na aaye.
Conclusion
Sahi ethical rules ke saath AI, India aur Odisha ke anmol gyan ko aane wali generations ke liye
safe rakh sakta hai.
